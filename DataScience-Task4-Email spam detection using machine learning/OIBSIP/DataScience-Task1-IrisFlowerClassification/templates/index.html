Meet the Iris Species

Setosa

Setosa generally has smaller petals and distinct measurements that make it easier to distinguish from the other two species.

Versicolor

Versicolor has intermediate measurements and shares some feature overlap with Virginica.

Virginica

Virginica generally has larger petal measurements compared with the other Iris species.

Which Features Are Most Discriminative?

The Iris dataset contains four physical measurements: sepal length, sepal width, petal length and petal width. Petal measurements generally provide strong separation between the species. The feature-importance chart above is calculated from the trained Random Forest model.

Project Objective
